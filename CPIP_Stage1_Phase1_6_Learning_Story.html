What I Built

In Phase 1.6, I built the fourth CPIP agent — the Resume Readiness Agent. This agent evaluates whether a student's resume has all required sections (education, skills, projects, contact details, role alignment) and is interview-ready.

Career & Placement Domain Learning

I learned that a complete, well-written resume is the first gate to interviews.

Key Insight:

Recruiters spend seconds scanning resumes. A resume missing sections or lacking role alignment gets rejected immediately. A resume with all required sections and clear role alignment passes the first gate and gets an interview invitation.

Why Resume Matters in Placement

A complete resume includes: education proof, clear skills list, real project experience, contact details, and role alignment. This is what recruiters need to understand if a candidate is worth an interview.

Agentic AI Learning

I learned that agentic systems evaluate data against requirements and classify readiness.

The Evaluation Principle:

The Resume Readiness Agent checks each resume section (Education: Present/Missing, Skills: Present/Missing, etc.) and counts how many are present. This count becomes a score. This score helps downstream agents understand readiness without re-evaluating the resume.
