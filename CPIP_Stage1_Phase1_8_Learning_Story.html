🎤 CPIP Learning Story

Stage 1 — Phase 1.8
Interview Readiness Agent

What I Built

In Phase 1.8, I built the sixth CPIP agent — the Interview Readiness Agent. This agent evaluates interview preparedness across 4 dimensions: aptitude (reasoning), technical (coding), communication (clarity), and project explanation (ability to discuss work).

The agent produces a composite interview readiness score and identifies weak dimensions for focused preparation.

Career & Placement Domain Learning

I learned that interviews are the final gate to placement.

Key Insight:

Even students with strong skills, portfolios, and resumes fail interviews due to weak communication, inability to explain projects, or poor aptitude preparation. The Interview Readiness Agent evaluates all dimensions that matter in interviews.

Agentic AI Learning

I learned that agentic systems aggregate multi-dimensional evaluations into composite readiness.

The Aggregation Principle:

The Interview Readiness Agent takes 4 separate dimension scores (aptitude, technical, communication, project) and combines them into a single readiness assessment. This shows how complex evaluations are synthesized in agentic systems.

Implementation

Two API Endpoints:

GET /interview-readiness/1 - Interview readiness for student 1

Returns score, dimension breakdown, and weak areas for improvement

GET /interview-readiness - All students' interview readiness

3 students evaluated across all dimensions

Validation

✅ API Response: GET /interview-readiness → 200 OK

✅ Dimension Analysis: All 4 dimensions scored

✅ Composite Score: Average of dimensions calculated

✅ Weak Dimensions: Identified for improvement focus
