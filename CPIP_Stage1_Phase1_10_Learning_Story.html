Stage 1 — Phase 1.10
Job Opportunity Matching

What I Built

In Phase 1.10, I built the eighth CPIP agent — the Job Opportunity Agent. This agent matches students to actual job openings by comparing student skills and readiness with job requirements and eligibility criteria.

It evaluates skill fit (required + preferred), checks eligibility (CGPA, degree), and produces a ranked list of suitable job opportunities with fit scores.

Career & Placement Domain Learning

I learned that job matching is NOT only skill matching.

Key Insight:

A student may have good skills but be ineligible due to CGPA or degree requirements. A student may be eligible but lack critical skills. The Job Opportunity Agent evaluates BOTH skill fit AND eligibility to determine if a job is truly suitable.

Agentic AI Learning

I learned that agentic systems match complex multi-criterion entities.

The Matching Principle:

The Job Opportunity Agent compares student profiles with job requirements across multiple dimensions: required skills, preferred skills, CGPA, readiness scores. This shows how agents can evaluate complex matching scenarios.

Implementation

Two API Endpoints:

GET /job-match/1 - Job matches for student 1

Returns all jobs, suitable jobs, and fit scores

GET /job-match - Job matches for all students

Summary showing suitable job counts per student
