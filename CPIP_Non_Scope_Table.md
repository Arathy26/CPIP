| What CPIP Does NOT Do | Why |
|---|---|
| Does not guarantee placement | Job outcomes depend on market conditions and employer decisions, not CPIP. |
| Does not make final hiring decisions | Hiring decisions belong to employers, not CPIP. |
| Does not invent or create skills | Skill development is the student's responsibility, not CPIP's. |
| Does not replace mentors | CPIP supports mentors, but final decisions remain in the mentor's hands. |
| Does not write or create resume | CPIP cannot write a resume, but it can suggest improvements. |
