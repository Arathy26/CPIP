CPIP helps students understand if they're ready for their aimed job and what they need to improve. 
Students often don't know what skills match their target job, and they apply without understanding 
the gaps in their resume, portfolio or interview readiness. This creates frustration and wasted effort.

CPIP is different because it uses deterministic agents and clear rules. CPIP shows you how ready 
you are for a job and why you match it, so you know the exact reason instead of just being told. 
But humans still make the final decision.

This matters because students, mentors and placement officers can get the exact idea on what is 
missing instead of guessing.
